🛡️ Safety Information

Important safety instructions to follow during waterlogging and heavy rainfall.

🛡️

General Safety

  • Stay away from heavily waterlogged areas.
  • Avoid unnecessary travel during severe rainfall.
  • Follow AQUANEX risk alerts and warnings.
  • Move to a safer location if water levels continue to rise.
🚗

Road and Vehicle Safety

  • Avoid driving through deep or rapidly rising water.
  • Do not enter roads where the water depth cannot be determined.
  • Reduce vehicle speed during heavy rainfall.
  • Avoid low-lying roads and underpasses during waterlogging.
⚡

Electrical Safety

  • Stay away from exposed electrical wires.
  • Do not touch electrical equipment while standing in water.
  • Avoid areas where electrical poles or cables may be damaged.
  • Report dangerous electrical conditions to the appropriate authorities.
🌧️

Heavy Rainfall Precautions

  • Monitor weather conditions regularly.
  • Check AQUANEX alerts when rainfall increases.
  • Keep important documents and electronic devices in safe locations.
  • Avoid unnecessary movement through flood-prone areas.
🚨

Emergency Response

  • Follow instructions from emergency authorities.
  • Move to higher and safer areas when required.
  • Do not attempt to cross rapidly flowing water.
  • Contact local emergency services when immediate assistance is required.
